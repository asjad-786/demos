About

How we work

We start with what has come into the cooler that morning. Peonies in spring, dahlias in late summer, branches and berries once the weather turns. The arrangement follows the flowers, not the other way around, so what you take home is shaped by what looks its best that week.

Tell us who the flowers are for and where they will sit. We will ask about the room, the light and how long they need to last, then make something to suit it. Nothing leaves the shop until we would be happy to hand it over ourselves.
