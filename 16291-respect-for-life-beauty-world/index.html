About

How we work

We take the time to ask what you want before we pick up the clippers. A good cut starts with a few questions about how you wear your hair and how much upkeep you can manage, and we'd rather get that right than rush to the finish.

The shop is a place to sit down and get it done properly. We work with the hair you have, keep the tools clean and sharp, and send you out the door with a cut you can recreate at home.

Careful, unhurried work

We take the time to get the lines and blend right, not just get you out the door.

Sharp tools, clean chair

Blades, clippers and towels are kept clean and sharp for every client.
