About

How we work

Every arrangement is built by hand, one stem at a time. We pick flowers for their condition and color, not for a set recipe, so what you receive reflects the season and the week we had. Tell us the occasion, the room it will sit in, and how long it needs to last, and we will shape the design around that.

We keep things simple. You get a clear quote before anything is made, honest advice about what will hold up in your space, and flowers that look like someone cared about them. If something is not right, tell us and we will fix it. We would rather make one thing well than many things quickly.
