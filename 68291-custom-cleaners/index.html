About

How we work

We wash, dry, fold, and press at our Irving shop, and we treat every order like it matters. Shirts get checked for loose buttons and stains before they go in the machine. Sheets go through the dryer on a gentler heat so they last.

Dry cleaning, stain work, and bedding get the same attention. If something needs a second look, we tell you before it goes out, not after. Pick a pickup window that suits you and we will take it from there.

Careful handling

Each piece is checked for stains, buttons, and fabric care before it is washed.
