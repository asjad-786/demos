About

How we cook

We keep the menu focused on what the grill does best. Ribs and chicken go on low and slow until the meat pulls clean, then get a hot finish so the outside catches color and smoke. Every plate is built around that char, with simple sides that let it speak.

The room is meant for an easy meal, whether you are stopping in after work or bringing the whole table. Ask your server what came off the grill that day, and take a little extra salsa verde home. We would rather you leave full and comfortable than in a hurry.

Cooked over real heat

Meats are marinated, then charred on the grill for smoke and a crisp edge.

Easy, unhurried dining

A comfortable room on Buford Highway, good for a quick lunch or a long dinner.
