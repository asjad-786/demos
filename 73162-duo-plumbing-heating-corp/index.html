About

How we work

We work on the pipes and boilers that people in New York live with every day, in older walk-ups, prewar buildings and rowhouses where nothing is quite where the plans say it is. We'd rather look first and talk it through than guess and send you a bill for the guess.

When we arrive, we'll show you what we found, explain the options in plain terms and tell you what each one costs. If you say go, we protect your floors, do the work carefully and clean up before we leave. If you'd rather think it over, that's fine too.
