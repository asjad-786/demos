About

Slow cuts, sharp lines

I cut hair the way I'd want mine cut. We talk through what you want first, whether that's a photo on your phone or just a rough idea. Then I check the shape from every side before the last pass, so it works with your hair instead of fighting it.

The shop sits on West Bethany Home Road. Walk-ins are welcome when a chair is free, and booking ahead keeps the wait short. Bring your questions. There's no rush in the chair, and you'll leave knowing how to keep the cut looking good at home.
