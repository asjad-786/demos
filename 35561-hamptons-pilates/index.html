About

How we teach

We keep classes small so every instructor can see your hips, your ribs and where your weight sits. Cues are plain and specific, and we slow things down when a movement starts to hurry. You will leave each session knowing more about how your body moves than when you walked in.

Most people come in with a tight back, a sore hip or a plain wish to feel steadier. We start there, not with a routine copied from a poster. Whether you are new to Pilates or returning after time away, the work meets you where you are and builds from it.

Small classes

Limited spots mean your instructor can watch your form and adjust it as you move.

Clear, patient cues

We explain the why behind each movement so the work carries over outside the studio.

Options for every body

Group, duet and private sessions suit beginners, returning clients and people managing an old injury.
