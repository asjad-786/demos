About

Baked on North Broadway

We bake in small batches, so the conchas come out with a crackly sugar top and the cakes are frosted close to pickup. Most custom orders start with a conversation about who the cake is for and what they like to eat.

Tell us the date, the guest count and the flavors you love. We'll suggest a size and filling that fits, and we'll be straight with you if a design needs more time than you have. Stop by the counter for a pastry, or call ahead for a cake.

Small-batch baking

Pastries are baked in small batches, so each tray comes out fresh and soft.

Honest cake planning

We'll tell you how much cake you need and which fillings hold up well.

Classic and custom

Pan dulce at the counter, and cakes designed around your celebration.
