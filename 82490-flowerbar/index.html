About

How we work

We start with the flowers that look best right now. Stems change as the seasons turn, so the palette moves from early spring tulips to late dahlias. We would rather tell you what is good this week than sell you something tired.

Every arrangement is built by hand at the bench with the vase, the room, and the person receiving it in mind. Tell us where it will sit and how long it needs to last, and we will take it from there. Ask us about pickup and delivery.
