About

How we work on your car

We start by listening to what the car is doing. A grinding stop, a light that won't go off, an AC that quits in the middle of August. We check it properly, show you what we found, and quote the work before anything gets started.

We'd rather fix the one thing that's actually wrong than sell you a list of extras. If a part can wait, we'll tell you and write it down. Bring the car in, ask as many questions as you like, and take your time deciding.

Written estimates first

We explain the problem and quote the work before we pick up a wrench.

Straight answers on cost

Built for Houston heat

Batteries, AC and tires work hard in Houston summers. We see that every week.
