About

How we coach

We keep the workouts varied on purpose, so no two days ask the same thing of your body. Each class opens with a skill or lift, then a workout built around it. You work at a weight and pace that fits you, and a coach is on the floor correcting your setup before a bad rep becomes a bad habit.

Most people come in not knowing what a clean is, and that is fine. We teach the movement first and add load once it looks right. Whether you are back after a long break or chasing a first pull-up, the goal is the same: to leave a little stronger than you arrived and to keep coming back.

Coaches on the floor

Someone is watching your form every rep, so you learn to move well from the start.

Scaled to you

Every workout has versions for beginners and experienced lifters, so everyone trains hard.

Varied, never boring

Different movements and stimuli each day keep training fresh and progress steady.
