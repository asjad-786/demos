About

A studio that feels like a neighborhood

We teach yoga as something to share. In our classes you will find people of different ages, bodies and experience levels practicing side by side. Every class comes with clear cues and options, so you can work at the edge of your own ability without feeling left behind.

Whether it is your first time on a mat or you have been practicing for years, come as you are. Bring your water, arrive a few minutes early if you can, and leave the rest to us. We keep the lights low and the pace honest.

Every level welcome

Clear options in every class so beginners and regulars can practice together.
