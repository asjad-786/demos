About

How we work

We keep things simple. You get a clear explanation of what the car needs, why it needs it, and what it will cost before we touch anything. If a part can wait, we will say so. If it cannot, we will show you the worn piece.

Most of what we do is routine work done properly: fresh oil, tires that wear evenly, brakes that stop when you ask them to. We give a quick lube the same care as a full repair, and we would rather you leave understanding your car a little better than you came in.

Clear quotes first

We explain the work and price before starting. No surprises at pickup.

Show, don't just tell

Worn parts are shown to you when you ask, so the recommendation makes sense.

Easy to reach
