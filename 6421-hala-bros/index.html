About

About the kitchen

We cook the food we like to eat and keep the portions generous enough to share. The grill runs hot, the plates leave the kitchen hot, and we would rather send out less food than food that has been sitting around.

Most of what we do is simple: good ingredients, careful cooking, and tables that stay full long enough for people to catch up. Come in with a group or grab something to take home. Either way, we want you to leave full.
