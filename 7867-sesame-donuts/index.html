About

Small batches, short menu

We keep the menu short so each donut gets proper attention. The dough is mixed, proofed, and fried in small batches, and the sesame glaze goes on while the ring is still warm so it sets thin and crackles a little at the edge.

Stop in for one donut or take a box home. Ask what came out of the fryer last, and we will tell you what is best right now.
