About

How we wash

We run Ducky's the way we'd want our own cars cleaned. Every wash starts with a pre-soak and a close look at the lower panels, where salt and grit collect first. Then it's foam, rinse and dry, with extra time on the wheels and the spots that need it.

Some days you need ten minutes and a quick rinse. Other days the interior has seen one too many coffee cups and a winter's worth of sand. Tell us what the car has been through, and we'll tell you which wash fits.
