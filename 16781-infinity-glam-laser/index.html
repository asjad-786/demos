About

A small room, done properly

We keep the book intentionally light so each client gets real time in the chair. Consultations come first, so we talk through what you want, what your hair or skin is doing right now, and what will hold up between visits.

Laser is a medical-style treatment, so we explain the process, test a small area before anything else, and set expectations honestly. Hair and skin work is about patience and small adjustments, and we would rather tell you the truth than oversell a result.

Consultation first

We talk through your goals and your hair or skin before any product or laser touches you.

Honest timelines

You will hear how many sessions or visits a result realistically takes, not a best-case promise.

Calm, private rooms

Quiet chairs and unhurried appointments, so you can relax and actually enjoy the time.
