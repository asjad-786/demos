About

Cooking that stays simple

We cook the food we would want to eat on an ordinary weeknight, not a showpiece. Vegetables get the attention they deserve, meat comes off the heat with a good char, and the sauces and dressings are made in the kitchen rather than poured from a bottle.

The bar works the same way. Drinks are balanced rather than loud, and the bartenders will walk you through the list or leave you alone with your glass. Come in for one plate or one drink, and stay as long as the evening wants.

Cooked to order

Every plate leaves the pass when it's ready, not when the room is full.
