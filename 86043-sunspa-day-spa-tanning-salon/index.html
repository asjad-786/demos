About

How we work

We keep the room quiet and the towels warm. Most visits start with a short talk about what your skin or your week needs, so we pick the treatment together instead of steering you to the priciest option.

Tanning and spray tans sit alongside the facials and massages, so you can come in for a glow before a trip or a slow Saturday to yourself. Book ahead when you can. We keep the schedule tight so you aren't sitting in a lobby.
