About

How we cook

We cook Mediterranean food the way it is meant to be eaten: shared, unhurried, and built on good oil, fresh herbs, and bread that still arrives warm. Every plate starts with a few simple ingredients and a clear idea of how they should taste.

The menu moves with the market and the season, so some dishes change more than others. Ask your server what is best that night. We would rather you order three plates to share and stay for one more glass than rush you toward the check.
