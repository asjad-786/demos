About

A tailor's shop for women's clothes

We work on women's clothing every day, from a hem that needs to clear a new pair of heels to a gown that has to be right by the ceremony. Bring the piece in and we'll talk through what it needs before anything gets cut or pinned.

We like to see you in the garment with the shoes or undergarments you plan to wear. Fit depends on those details, so we take our time at the mirror and explain each change as we make it.

Fitted to you

We pin and check on your body, not a size chart, before anything is finished.

Clear pricing

We quote each job at the counter so you know the cost before we start.

Close to home

Located on 72nd Street in Jackson Heights, easy to reach on foot or by train.
