About

Cooked to order, one plate at a time

We cook each dish when you order it, so the herbs stay fresh and the wok stays hot. Curry pastes are balanced by hand, and the sauces are built to taste like Thai food should: sour, salty, sweet and spicy in the same bite.

Come in for a relaxed lunch or a longer dinner with friends. Share a few plates, or take a curry home for an easy weeknight meal. Tell us how spicy you like things and we will cook to that.
