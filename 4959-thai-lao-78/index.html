About

How we cook

We cook the way the food is meant to be eaten: pounded, toasted, and balanced between sour, salty, sweet, and hot. Herbs go in at the last minute so they stay bright, and the rice is steamed to order so it stays soft and chewy.

Thai and Lao cooking shares a lot, and we like showing both. Spice is up to you, and if you're not sure where to start, ask us what the kitchen is proudest of that day.

Cooked to order

Salads are pounded and noodles are fried when you order, so the textures stay sharp.

Heat on your terms

Tell us how spicy you like it, from mild and easy to properly hot.

Honest, fresh flavors

Lime, lemongrass, and fresh herbs do the work, with no heavy shortcuts.
