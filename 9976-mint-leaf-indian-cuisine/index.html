About

How we cook

Everything starts with the spice. We toast whole cumin, coriander and cardamom in the pan, grind them fresh, and let onions and tomatoes cook down slowly until the sauce turns deep and glossy. It takes longer that way, and you can taste it.

Breads and kebabs come off the clay oven, so the edges char and the insides stay soft. Tell us how hot you like your food and we will meet you there. Ask your server about vegetarian dishes or anything you would like adjusted.

Spices ground in-house

We toast and grind whole spices ourselves, so every sauce tastes of its ingredients.

Real clay-oven cooking

Naan, paneer tikka and kebabs come straight from the tandoor, blistered and fresh.

Heat level, your call

Mild to hot, with plenty of vegetarian dishes. Tell us how you like it.
