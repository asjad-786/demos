About

How we work

We take one dog at a time, start to finish. That means a quiet table, a dryer you can hear coming, and enough time to dry a double coat properly instead of rushing it into a crate.

Nervous dogs get a slower start. We talk through your dog's coat, habits and the look you want before we touch a clipper, and we'll tell you plainly if something needs a vet first.

No rushed slots

Each dog gets a table and our full attention.

Gentle with nervous dogs

Slow introductions, soft tools and breaks when a dog needs one.

Clear pricing up front

We confirm the price for your dog's size and coat before we start.
