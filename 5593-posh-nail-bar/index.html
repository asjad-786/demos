About

How we work

We keep the menu simple so each appointment gets proper time. Cuticles are softened, nails are shaped to suit your hands, and polish goes on in thin, even coats. If a shape will not suit your nail bed, we will say so and suggest something that will.

Every tool is cleaned between clients, and we would rather book you a little later than rush a set. Come in for a fill, a full set, or a quiet hour with your feet up. Bring a photo if you have one and we will work from it.

Time to do it right

Each booking gives your nails room to be shaped and finished properly.

Clean, careful tools

Implements are cleaned between clients and kept ready for the next appointment.

Easy to reach

On Interstate 35 in south Austin, easy to reach from the highway.
