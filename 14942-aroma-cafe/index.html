About

How we work

We keep the menu short so every drink and plate gets proper attention. Espresso is pulled fresh, milk is steamed by hand, and the bake case is refilled through the morning rather than filled once and forgotten.

Come in for a quick flat white before work or stay for a slow afternoon with a book. The room is quiet enough to talk, bright enough to read, and we are glad to see you either way.

Espresso pulled to order

Every shot is made when you order, tuned daily for balance.

Baked through the morning

The pastry case refills in small batches, so what you pick is recently baked.

Good light, easy seating

A bright room and a pace that never feels rushed.
