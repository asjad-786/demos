About

Made slowly, served plainly

We keep the menu short so we can do each drink properly. Beans are dialed in by hand, milk is steamed to order, and the grinder gets cleaned before the shop opens. Good coffee comes from paying attention to small things, and that is most of what we do here.

Come in for a quick espresso on the way to work, or stay with a pour-over and a book. The room is made for both. We would rather you sit a while than rush out, and we will keep the coffee coming while you do.

Small, focused menu

A short list of drinks and bakes means each one gets the attention it deserves.

Coffee brewed to order

Espresso is pulled and filter coffee is poured fresh, not left sitting on a warmer.

A room to stay in

Good light, quiet corners, and enough space to settle in with your laptop or a paper.
