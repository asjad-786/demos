About

Careful work, no rush

We cut hair and shape beards for people who notice the details: a line that sits square, a fade that blends without a visible band, a shave that doesn't sting. Every visit starts with a short conversation about what you want, so you're not guessing from a photo on the wall.

The beauty side of the shop gets the same attention. Brow shaping and facial waxing are done slowly and carefully, and you can book them alongside a haircut so one trip covers it all. Come in, sit down, and leave looking like you planned it.
