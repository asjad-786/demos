About

Slow hands, clean finish

We run appointments at the pace of the animal in front of us. Some dogs need a few minutes to sniff around before the dryer goes on, and some need a break halfway through. We build that time in, so nobody is rushed and nothing gets missed.

We talk through the cut before we start, and we tell you what we see on the skin and coat as we go. You will know what we did, why we did it, and what to watch for at home between visits.

Time to settle

Appointments have room for a nervous dog to warm up before clippers come out.

Gentle, patient handling

Calm voices, careful hands, and breaks whenever a pet needs one.

Clear plan, clear price

We agree on the cut and the price before we start, so there are no surprises at pickup.
