About

Simple food, cooked with attention

We keep the menu short so every pie gets the attention it needs. The dough rests, the sauce stays bright, and the cheese goes on generously. When something is not right, we would rather pull it than send it out.

Come in for a booth after work, or grab a pie to take home for the couch. Either way, the food should taste like someone cared about it. We try to make that easy, whether you are eating in the warm evening or on the patio with a cold drink.

Short, focused menu

A few things done well, rather than a long list we cannot keep up with.

Pies made to order

Your pizza is stretched and baked when you order, so it arrives hot and crisp.

Easy to share

Big pies, sides for the table, and a menu that works for families and groups.
