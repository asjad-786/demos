About

How we work

We keep the work simple: sharp clippers, clean blades, and enough time to get the fade right. Every chair gets the same attention, whether you come in for a quick line-up or a full hot towel shave.

Tell us what you wear to work and what you do on weekends, and we will cut for that. We would rather give you a haircut you can maintain at home than one that only looks right for a day.

Clean, precise lines

Cuts for your routine

Unhurried chair time
