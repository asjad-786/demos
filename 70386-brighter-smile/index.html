About

Careful dentistry, close to home

We keep appointments unhurried and explain each step before we start, so you know what's being done and why. Exams look at the whole mouth, not only the tooth that hurts, and treatment plans come with options and honest costs.

Our office on North Milwaukee Avenue suits an ordinary day: a stop before work, or a quick visit with the kids after school. Tell us what's bothering you, and we'll take it from there.

Plain-language treatment plans

Options and costs explained clearly before anything begins, so there are no surprises.

Gentle care, no rush

Take it slow, ask questions, and ask us to pause whenever you need to.

Easy to reach

A north side office for checkups, urgent visits, and the whole family.
