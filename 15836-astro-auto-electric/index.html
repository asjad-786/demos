About

How we work

Electrical problems are easy to guess at and expensive to guess wrong. We start with a meter, a wiring diagram, and a few questions about when the problem happens. Then we test until we know what is failing and why.

We explain each finding before we touch anything, and we show you the part or the connection that caused it. If a repair can wait, we say so. If it can't, we tell you what it will cost and what happens if you drive on it.

Test before replacing

We confirm the fault with measurements first, so you only pay for parts the car actually needs.

Plain-language explanations

You get a clear answer on what failed, why it failed, and what your options are.

Clean, lasting connections

Wiring repairs are soldered and sealed, not wrapped in tape and left to corrode again.
