About

Slow, clean, and a little playful

We start with a conversation. Placement, size, and the story behind a piece matter as much as the line itself, so we sketch together before anything goes on skin. Good tattoos are planned, not rushed.

The studio runs on fresh needles, clean surfaces, and steady hands. Whether you want a small fine line or a full back piece, you will get a straight answer on what will work on your skin and how to look after it.

Designed with you

Every piece starts from a sketch you can change before the session.

Clean, careful setup

Single-use supplies, disinfected surfaces, and an unhurried pace in the chair.

Honest aftercare

Clear healing guidance so your tattoo stays sharp for years.
