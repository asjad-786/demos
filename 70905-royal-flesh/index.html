About

How we work

Every piece starts with a real conversation. We ask where it will sit, how big you want it, and what it means to you, then we draw until the design fits the body it lives on. You get straight answers on timing and price before you sit down.

The room stays calm and clean, and we keep the pace steady so you can focus on the work and not the clock. After your session we walk you through aftercare so the piece heals sharp and stays that way for years.

Designs made for you

Custom work shaped around your body, placement, and idea, not pulled off a wall.

Clear pricing up front

We talk through size, time, and cost before you book, so there are no surprises.

Calm, careful sessions

A steady pace, a clean setup, and aftercare guidance you can follow at home.
