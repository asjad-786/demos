About

Cooked the slow way

Good Mexican food takes time. The sauces are built in layers, the meats marinate overnight, and the tortillas get pressed and toasted while you wait. We cook the way the food asks to be cooked, not the way that's fastest.

Come in hungry and take your time. Order a few plates to share, pull a warm tortilla through the salsa, and let the kitchen do the rest. We want the table to feel like a家 kitchen on a busy weekend, loud and generous and full of good smells.

Sauces made from scratch

Moles, salsas, and marinades are cooked in-house, built on dried chiles and fresh herbs.

Tortillas done right

Corn and flour tortillas are served warm, the way they should be eaten.

Easy on Sandy Boulevard

A relaxed room on Northeast Sandy, a good place for a casual dinner or a quick lunch.
