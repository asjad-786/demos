About

Lift like you mean it, with someone watching

We run Iron Forge for people who came to lift and want the room to back them up. Every session has a reason behind it: a warm-up that prepares you, main lifts that matter, and accessory work that fills the gaps. If you are new to the barbell, we teach the movement before we add weight.

Our coaches care more about how you move than how much you load. Expect direct feedback, a plan you can follow on the days you would rather skip, and a gym where your progress is the point. Nobody here is performing for the mirror.

Built around the barbell

Programming and coaching centered on lifting well, not on machines or trends.

Coaches who watch your form

Cues on your setup, bar path, and breathing, every session, from people paying attention.

A room that stays focused

Train hard, mind your own sets, and leave with a clear next step for the week.
