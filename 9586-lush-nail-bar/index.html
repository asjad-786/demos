About

A slower kind of nail appointment

We keep the schedule loose enough that nobody is rushed through a cuticle or a color change. Good nail work takes patience, and the best results come from taking the time to file, buff, and cure each layer properly.

Bring in a photo, a shade you saw on someone else, or nothing at all. We will talk through what suits your nails and your week, then pick colors together. You leave with a set that looks the way you pictured it.

Clear prices up front

You will know the cost and timing of your service before the first file touches your nail.

Patient, careful application

Each layer is prepped, thinned, and cured properly, so polish lasts and nails stay healthy.

Color help when you need it

Not sure what shade to pick? We will suggest options that suit your skin tone and style.
