About

Cooked over charcoal, one skewer at a time

We keep the menu focused on what the grill does well. Meat is marinated in yogurt, lemon, and onion before it goes on the skewer, then turned over live coals until the edges char and the center stays moist.

Every plate comes with rice, grilled tomato, fresh herbs, and bread, so the meal feels complete without extra ordering. It is simple food, cooked with attention, and we would rather get the basics right than make the menu longer.

Cooked over real charcoal

Skewers hit open coals, so the edges char and the inside stays juicy.

Full plates, every time
