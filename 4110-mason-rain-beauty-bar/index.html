About

How we work

Every visit starts with a short talk about your hair, how much time you spend on it, and what you want it to look like day to day. That conversation matters more than any photo, and it's how we get the cut right the first time.

We like the shop to be a place to slow down. Towels are warm, shaves get the time they need, and nobody gets rushed out of the chair. Book ahead if you have a set time, and come in when you can if you don't.
