About

How we work

We keep the menu short so everything can be made properly. Biscuits are mixed in the morning and baked in batches, so they come out hot rather than sitting under a lamp. Espresso gets tasted and adjusted before the first cup goes out.

We want the place to feel like a good kitchen counter: unhurried, a little loud in the best way, and simple to order from. Ask us what's fresh and we'll tell you honestly what we think you'll like.

Baked in small batches

Biscuits and pastries come out in batches through the morning, so they arrive warm.

Coffee dialed in daily

Espresso is tasted and adjusted before service, and the pour-over menu changes often.

A short, focused menu

Fewer items means each one gets the attention it needs, from the first bite to the last.
