About

How we work

We build each arrangement by hand, starting with what is freshest that day. Stems are conditioned, cut to length and placed one at a time, so the shape reads well from across the room and lasts longer at home.

Tell us who the flowers are for and what the moment is, and we will suggest a size and a palette that fits. Most people leave with something simpler and better than they expected.
