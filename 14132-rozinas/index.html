About

How we work

We keep the schedule unhurried so you are never rushed through a cut or color. Before we pick up the scissors, we look at your hair as it is today, ask how you style it at home, and talk through what will actually fit your mornings.

Good hair work is mostly listening and planning. We tell you honestly what your hair can take, suggest a plan for growing out color or keeping a cut in shape, and leave you with a few things you can do between visits.
