About

How we work

We keep the chair schedule short on purpose, so each cut gets the time it needs. You sit down, we talk through what you want, then we work from the back of the head forward and check the line from both sides before we hand you the mirror.

Most of the work is in the details: a neckline that grows out cleanly, a beard that looks like it was never trimmed on purpose. We would rather tell you a cut will take longer than rush it. Bring a photo if that helps, or just tell us what bugs you about your hair now.

Time in the chair

Appointments are booked with enough room that nobody is rushed through their cut.

Clean, sharp tools

Clippers, shears, and a straight razor, kept clean and sharp between every client.

Honest advice

We tell you what your hair can actually do before we cut.
