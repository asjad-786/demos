About

About the chair

I like to take my time with each person in the chair. Good barbering is part listening and part measuring, so I start by asking how you wear your hair and what you want it to do for you. The work is clean and deliberate. Clippers and scissors get the shape right, the razor sets the edges, and nothing leaves the chair until it sits the way you asked for it to.

Cuts planned with you

We talk through your hair, your routine and your style before the clippers start.

Details that hold up

Clean edges, even fades and a neckline that stays sharp well past the appointment.

Easy to book
