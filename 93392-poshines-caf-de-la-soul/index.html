About

Cooked slow, served plain

We cook the way our kitchen was taught to cook: greens simmered low, gravy made in the pan, cornbread mixed by hand. Nothing here is rushed, and nothing is hidden behind a fancy name. You order at the counter, find a seat by the window, and the food comes out hot.

The café is meant to be a place to sit a while. Bring a friend for brunch, grab a coffee before work, or pick up a tray for Sunday dinner. We want every plate to taste like someone cared about it, because someone did.

Cooked from scratch

Gravies, greens and cornbread are made in the kitchen each day, not reheated.

A real neighborhood spot

Easy to find on North Denver Avenue, with room to sit and linger.

Plates for every visit

Brunch, a quick lunch, takeout or a full catering tray, all from one kitchen.
