About

How we cook here

We start with what the market has and build the menu from there. Some weeks that means a long list of greens and stone fruit, other weeks it is root vegetables and whatever the fish supplier brings in. The menu changes often, and we would rather tell you what is new than keep a dish around because it sells.

The room is quiet enough to talk and warm enough to stay. Ask the team about wine, cocktails, or what they would eat on a given night. We are happy to help you choose, and we will leave the table alone when you want more time between courses.
