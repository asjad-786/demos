About

Soft ice, bright syrup, no rush

We care most about the texture. Shaved fine and soft, the ice soaks up syrup instead of crunching, so every spoonful tastes like the flavor you chose. Keep it simple or build a bigger cup with toppings. Either way, we will tell you what works well together.

The name means women of the sea in Hawaiian, and that is the feeling we want here: salty air, cold sweet ice, and a shady spot to sit. Take your time, bring the group, and come back for a second cup.
