About

Slow hands, quiet room

We work with one animal at a time and let each pet set the pace. We start with a calm introduction, take breaks when needed, and never hurry a blow-dry or a nail trim just to fit a schedule.

Shampoos and conditioners are chosen for coat type and skin, not just how they smell. If we notice something at a visit, like a lump, a rash or sore paws, we'll tell you when you pick up.

One pet at a time

Each appointment gets our full attention, start to finish, with no rushing between clients.

Products matched to coat

Shampoos and conditioners are matched to coat type and skin, not just scent.

Easy to reach

Drop-off and pickup on 86th Street in Brooklyn, close to home for most neighbors.
