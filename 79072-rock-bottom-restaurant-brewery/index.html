About

Beer brewed on site, food to match

We brew our beer in the same building where we serve it, so the pint in your hand was made right here. Lagers stay crisp and clean, the ales lean toward caramel and citrus, and the seasonals change with the weather and with what we feel like drinking.

The kitchen keeps things simple: good burgers, grilled meats, and sides that suit a long afternoon. Pull up a stool at the bar or settle into a booth with the people you came with, and let the food come out when it's ready.
