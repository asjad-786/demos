About

How we work

Most of the work here starts with a tape measure and a conversation. I want to know how you stand, what you wear on weekends, and where your jackets pull. Then we pick the cloth or leather together and I cut the pattern for your body, not for a size chart.

Shoes, belts and small leather goods come out of the same workroom, so a repair or a new pair of loafers gets the same attention as a full suit. Stop in for a fitting and we'll talk through what makes sense for the way you actually live.
