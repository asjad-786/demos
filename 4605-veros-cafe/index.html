About

How we work

We keep the menu small so each drink and plate gets proper attention. Espresso is pulled when you order, milk is steamed fresh, and food goes out when it is ready, not sitting under a lamp.

Vero's is a place to slow down on Commerce Street. Bring a laptop, meet a friend, or grab a cup on the way somewhere else. Tell us what you like, and we will point you toward something worth finishing.

Small, focused menu

Fewer items, made carefully, so each cup and plate tastes the way it should.

Drinks made to order

Espresso is pulled and milk steamed when you order, not batched ahead of time.

Easy to reach downtown

On West Commerce, good for a quick stop or a long morning.
