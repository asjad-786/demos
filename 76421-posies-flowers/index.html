About

How we work

We build every arrangement by hand, one stem at a time, starting with what looks best that week. Flowers change with the season, so the bouquet you order in spring will not look like the one you order in November. We would rather tell you that than fill a vase with stock that has been sitting around.

Tell us the occasion, who it is for, and roughly what you want to spend. We will suggest flowers and colors, then shape the piece to fit. Pickup and local delivery are both available, and for weddings and larger events we walk through the plan together before anything gets cut.
