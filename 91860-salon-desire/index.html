About

How we work

We keep the pace steady and the chair comfortable. You get our full attention for the whole appointment, and nothing gets rushed to make room for the next person. The goal is a cut that looks right when you walk out and still looks right a week later.

Most of what we do comes down to small details: the way a fade blends, how a beard line sits against the jaw, a neckline that doesn't grow out crooked. If something doesn't feel right, say so before you leave. We would rather fix it in the chair than hear about it later.

Lines that hold

Sharp edges and clean necklines that still look good through the week.

Time in the chair

Unhurried appointments, with room to talk or simply sit back and relax.

Easy to book
