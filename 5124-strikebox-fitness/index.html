About

How we train

Boxing is a good teacher because it is honest. Your feet, hands, and breathing show up in every round, and the bag does not care how you felt that morning. We build classes around that: clear combinations, plenty of footwork, and enough conditioning that you finish tired and a little proud of it.

Coaches here pay attention to the person in front of them. Newcomers get a slower start and clear form cues. Regulars get harder rounds and a reason to keep coming back. Whatever you are working toward, we will tell you what we see and help you get there without the noise.

Coaches who watch form

We correct your stance, guard, and footwork early, so good habits stick from the first class.

Boxing and lifting together

Bag rounds for conditioning and the strength floor for power, in one gym with one membership.

Start where you are

Beginners, returners, and experienced fighters all get classes pitched at their level.
