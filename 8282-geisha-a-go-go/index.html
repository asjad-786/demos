About

How we cook and host

We cook food we like to eat after a long day: bright, a little bold, and meant to be passed around. Plates arrive when they are ready, so your table can order in rounds and keep talking between bites.

The room is loud on purpose. Bring a crowd, grab a seat at the bar, or come in alone with a book. We would rather you stay for one more drink than rush out the door.
