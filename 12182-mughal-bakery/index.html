About

Made by hand, one batch at a time

We bake the slow way. Dough is mixed and rested, naan is shaped by hand and cooked until the edges blister and the middle stays soft. Everything goes out in small batches, so what is on the counter is usually still warm from the oven.

Cakes and trays are made to order, so the flavor, size and look get worked out with you before anything goes in the oven. Cardamom, rose, pistachio and saffron show up in the bakes we know best. Stop by the shop on Maplewood Avenue or send a note ahead, and we will take it from there.

Made by hand

Dough is shaped, rolled and baked in-house, one batch at a time.

Flavors with restraint

Cardamom, saffron, rose and pistachio, used lightly so each one comes through clearly.
