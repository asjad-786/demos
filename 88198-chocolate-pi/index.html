About

Chocolate, made slowly

We keep the menu small on purpose. Every pie, brownie and cookie starts with chocolate we would want to eat on its own, and we build the recipes around that flavor rather than sugar. When a batch isn't right, it doesn't leave the kitchen.

Come by the shop on North Florida Avenue for a slice and a cup of coffee, or order ahead for a holiday table or a birthday. Either way, we want the chocolate to taste like chocolate, and the crust to hold up to a fork.

Real chocolate first

Recipes start with good cocoa and dark chocolate, so the flavor comes through clearly.

A small, focused menu

We bake a handful of things well instead of trying to make everything.

Easy to order ahead

Pies, cakes and gift boxes can be reserved for holidays and celebrations.
