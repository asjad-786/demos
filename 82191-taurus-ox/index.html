About

Cooking at a pace that suits the table

We cook the way we like to eat: simply, with good ingredients, and with enough attention that each plate tastes like someone cared about it. The menu moves with the seasons and with what the market has on a given morning, so it is never quite the same twice.

Come in for a long dinner, a slow Sunday brunch, or a glass at the bar while you wait for a table. We want the room to feel easy, the food to arrive at a sensible pace, and the check to never feel like the end of the night.
