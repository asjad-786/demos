About

How we work

Most cars that come through our bay have one of two problems: something got hit, or something has been failing quietly until it stopped. We look at both the same way. We open the car up far enough to see what is actually wrong, then explain it in plain words before any work starts.

Collision work gets the same care as the mechanical side. Panels are straightened or replaced, paint is matched under good light, and the car is checked again before it goes back to you. You will know what was done and why.
