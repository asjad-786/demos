About

How we work

We cut hair slowly enough to get the details right and quickly enough that you don't lose the afternoon. Every visit starts with a real conversation about what you want and how you wear it day to day.

Bring a photo if it helps. We'll tell you honestly what your hair will do, then get to work with the right tool for the cut. The room stays relaxed, the music stays on, and nobody gets rushed out the door.
