About

How we run the place

We keep the machines running, the floors clean, and the counter open so you can get in, get your wash going, and get on with your day. Whether you want to do it yourself or hand it off, the goal is the same: clothes that look and smell the way they should.

For wash-and-fold, we sort by color and fabric, use the right temperature for each load, and fold everything the same way every time. Tell us about any stain or delicate piece when you drop it off, and we will take care of it with the care it needs.

Clean machines, every day

Sorted and folded with care
