About

A cafe that runs on routine

We keep the menu tight so every cup and plate gets attention. The espresso is dialed in each morning, the tortillas are pressed fresh, and the salsa is made the same way every day until we find a better version. Good neighborhood cafes are built on small habits like that.

Come in with a laptop, a book, or nothing at all. The chairs are comfortable, the music stays low, and the staff will remember how you like your coffee. We would rather you stay an hour than rush through a cup on the way out the door.

Carefully pulled espresso

Shots are dialed in each morning and adjusted through the day to keep the flavor steady.

Food made in the morning

Tortillas, salsa, and pastries are prepared in small batches before the doors open.

A room to settle into

Comfortable seating, low music, and enough space to work or catch up with a friend.
