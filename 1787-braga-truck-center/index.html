About

How we work

Most trucks come to us with one problem and a schedule to keep. We start by finding out what the truck is actually doing, then test it before we touch a wrench. That way the repair matches the fault, and you are not paying to replace parts that were fine.

We talk through the job before it starts: what needs doing, what it will cost, and how long the truck will be in the bay. If something turns up partway through, we call you first. Our goal is a truck that goes back out on time and runs right.
