About

How we work here

We start every cut with a conversation. We want to know what you want, how much time you spend on your hair and what you hope to look like on a Monday morning. Then we work slowly enough to get the details right, from the taper up to the part.

We like unhurried cuts and easy talk. Bring a reference photo or nothing at all. We'll tell you honestly what your hair can do, and we'll stop when it looks right, not when the clock says so.
