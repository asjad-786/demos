About

Cooked with patience

We cook the way we like to eat: slowly, with attention to salt, acid, and texture. Mornings start with coffee and bread, and the kitchen builds from there through lunch and into dinner. Nothing on the menu is meant to be rushed, and nothing should taste like it was.

The cafe is meant to be a place to sit. Bring a book, a friend, or a laptop, and take the time to enjoy what is on the plate and in the cup. We want the food to feel like a good meal at home, served on a Tuesday or a Saturday with the same care.

Cooked with care

Every plate is seasoned and finished with attention, from the first cup of coffee to the last bite.

Room to linger

A comfortable place to sit for a slow breakfast, a long lunch, or a conversation over dessert.

Easy to reach downtown

Set on Forsyth Street in the heart of Atlanta, it is an easy stop before or after your day.
