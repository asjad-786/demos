About

How we cook

We cook Italian food the way it is made at a family table: simply, with good ingredients and no unnecessary fuss. Pasta is rolled by hand, sauces are given the time they need, and nothing leaves the kitchen that we would not eat ourselves.

The menu changes with the season and with what looks best at the market that week. We would rather serve a few dishes done well than a long list we cannot keep up with. Come in hungry, order a few things to share, and stay for another glass.

Pasta made by hand

Dough rolled and cut in our kitchen, then cooked to order for every plate.

Sauces cooked slowly

Tomato, ragù and garlic given the patient time that good Italian cooking needs.

Room for any night

Easy for a quick lunch, a long dinner with friends or a small celebration.
