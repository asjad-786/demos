About

How we cook

We cook with ingredients and methods that have been part of Indigenous food traditions for generations. Corn, squash, greens, wild herbs, and careful preparation shape what comes out of the kitchen, and we let the season decide the rest.

Our goal is simple: food that tastes like it came from somewhere. We keep the menu honest, the portions generous, and the pace unhurried, so every meal feels like sitting down to dinner rather than rushing through one.
