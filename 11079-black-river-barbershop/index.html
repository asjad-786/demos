About

How we work

We keep the shop simple: a good chair, sharp tools and time to do each cut properly. We ask a few questions before we pick up the clippers, because the right cut depends on how you wear your hair day to day, not just what looks good in a photo.

Our work is about the details: even fades, clean edges and a beard line that holds up through the week. We would rather take an extra five minutes to get it right than rush you out the door. If something is not sitting right, tell us and we will fix it before you leave.
