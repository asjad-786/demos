About

How we work

Powder coating is mostly prep. Every part gets cleaned, stripped where needed and checked for rust or damage before any powder goes on. Skip that step and finishes peel, so we don't skip it.

Bring us a part or a set of wheels and we'll look it over with you. You'll get a clear price, a rough timeline, and a straight answer if something won't coat well. We'd rather say that up front than hand back a bad finish.
