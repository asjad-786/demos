About

How we cook

We cook the way a good market stall sells: what is ripe, what is local, and what tastes like itself. The menu gets rewritten as ingredients arrive, so a dish you love in spring may return only once its season comes back around.

Most of our work is simple on paper and careful at the stove. We salt early, cook over steady heat, and send plates out when they are ready, not when the clock says so. We would rather you leave full and unhurried than impressed by a long list.

Cooked to the market
