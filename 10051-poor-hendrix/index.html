About

How we cook

We keep the menu short so every plate gets attention. We cook with what looks best at the market that week and let the flavors do the talking, with more salt, more heat, and more time at the stove than the room might expect.

We want the room to feel like a dinner at a friend's table: unhurried, a little loud, and easy to stay in for another round. Bring your people, order too much, and take what's left home.
