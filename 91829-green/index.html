About

Slow hands, clean lines

At Green we treat each cut as its own job. We ask how you wear your hair on a normal day, look at the crown and the cowlick, and only then pick up the clippers. The goal is a cut that still looks right after a week of ignoring it.

The shop is a quiet place to sit down. Clippers are cleaned between clients, the towels are warm for shaves, and the conversation goes wherever you want it to, or nowhere at all. We'd rather you leave feeling settled than rushed.

Unhurried, careful cuts

Straight-razor shaves
