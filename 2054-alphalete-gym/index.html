About

A gym for people who lift

We built Alphalete around the basics: a clean lifting floor, solid racks, plenty of chalk and bar space, and enough room that you are not waiting on a squat rack between sets. Whether you are chasing a first pull-up or a bigger deadlift, the equipment should get out of your way.

Coaching is where we spend the most time. We watch every rep we can, correct the small things that cost you weight, and write plans you can actually follow on a Tuesday night. Bring your goals and your schedule, and we will work from there.

Room to work

Open lifting space and racks, so you spend your time under the bar, not waiting for it.

Coaching that watches

Coaches who look at your form, adjust your setup, and keep your training honest.

Plans that fit your week

Programs built around your goals and schedule, adjusted as you get stronger.
