About

How we work

Every tattoo starts with a conversation. We talk through what you want, where it will sit on your body, and how big it should be, then draw it out before anything is booked for the chair. If a design needs to change to read well on skin, we will tell you why and show you the options.

Piercing and tattoo work both depend on clean hands, clean tools, and a calm room. We keep the studio tidy, use fresh supplies for every client, and take the time to explain aftercare so you know what to expect once you walk out the door.

Designed with you
