About

How we work

We do car audio the way we'd want it done on our own cars. That means reading the wiring before pulling a panel, running power and ground properly, and leaving the dash looking like nothing was ever touched. We'd rather tell you what's worth spending on than sell you the biggest amp on the wall.

Every install gets a test before it leaves the bay. We play it at volume, check for rattles, look for heat at the connections, and set gains by ear and by meter. If something else in your car is causing trouble, like a weak ground or a failing alternator, we'll show you where it is and what it takes to fix it.
