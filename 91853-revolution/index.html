About

How we work

We keep the shop simple: a clean room, sharp tools, and a chair that stays yours for as long as the cut takes. Nobody gets rushed through a fade to make room for the next person.

Tell us what you want, then tell us what you do with your hair in the morning. That conversation matters more than any photo. We would rather get it right than get you out the door fast.

Time in the chair

Cuts take the time they need, not a rushed slot squeezed between appointments.

Clean, sharp tools

Clippers, blades and shears kept clean and sharp between every single client.

Easy to find

On Barbur Boulevard in Southwest Portland, straightforward to reach and simple to remember.
