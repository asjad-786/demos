About

How we work

We start every job by finding out what is actually wrong. You will hear what the problem is, what it will take to fix, and what can wait. Nothing gets done until you have approved the number.

Most of what comes through the bay is ordinary work done carefully: brakes that squeal, batteries that give out in July, check engine lights nobody has looked at yet. We keep the shop clean, explain the parts we use, and can show you the old ones if you want to see them.
