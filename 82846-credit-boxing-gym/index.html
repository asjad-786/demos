About

How we train

Boxing is a skill before it is a workout. We teach stance, footwork, and punching mechanics from the start, so that when you are tired in round three your hands still go where you send them. Classes are small enough that a coach can see your guard drop and fix it on the spot.

The room is simple: heavy bags, a ring, and a lot of mitts. Nobody needs a background in combat sports to walk in. Show up, wrap your hands, and we will take it one combination at a time. Most people leave sweaty, a little sore, and already planning the next session.

Coaches who correct you

Every round comes with feedback on stance, guard, and footwork, not just counting reps.

Small, focused classes

Group sizes stay modest so coaches can see your form and adjust it as you go.

Open to every level

Complete beginners and experienced fighters both get useful, specific work here.
