About

Made slowly, served plainly

We keep the menu small so each drink gets the attention it needs. Beans are dialed in every morning, milk is steamed by hand, and nothing sits under a heat lamp waiting for an order. If something is not right, we would rather remake it than hand it over.

Come in for a quick cortado on the way to work or stay for a slow pour-over and a book. The room is meant to be easy to sit in, with good light, quiet music, and staff who will tell you what they are drinking that day.

Made to order

Every drink is pulled, steamed, or brewed when you order it, not batched ahead.

A small, careful menu

We focus on a few drinks and bakes and make each one properly.

Room to linger

Good light, comfortable seats, and no rush to turn over your table.
