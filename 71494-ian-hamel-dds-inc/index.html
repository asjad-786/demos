About

How Dr. Hamel works

I like to explain what I see before I touch anything. You should know what the problem is, what your options are, and what each one costs before we begin. Most visits start with a conversation and a look at your X-rays, not with a drill.

Appointments are scheduled with room for questions and for the patients who need things to go slowly. If something can wait, I will say so. If it cannot, I will tell you plainly and we will make the time for it.

Plain-language explanations

Every treatment explained before we start, with the options and costs laid out.

Time for nervous patients

Appointments scheduled with room to ask questions and take things slowly.

Honest about what can wait

If a problem can wait, we say so. If it cannot, we act.
